# Notebook 16: Thesis Evidence and Empirical Reporting

## Purpose

Notebook 16 converts the certified empirical release into thesis-ready
tables, LaTeX fragments, figures and a compact numerical register.

It does not refit or reselect the model, continuous calibration,
event-probability calibration or trading strategy.

## Interpretation

The outputs preserve the empirical lineage certified in Notebook 15.
They are reporting artefacts rather than new empirical experiments.

The results are finite-sample and block-specific. They do not establish
executable profitability, population-level significance, universal
model superiority or market inefficiency.
