## Interpretation guard

The reported Delta and Gamma quantities are finite-difference sensitivities of event probabilities to local shifts in the predictive temperature location. They are Greek-like forecast-risk diagnostics, not Black-Scholes derivatives. TAEC PnL remains a snapshot-price mark-to-market convergence result, not a historical bid/ask fill-level backtest.
