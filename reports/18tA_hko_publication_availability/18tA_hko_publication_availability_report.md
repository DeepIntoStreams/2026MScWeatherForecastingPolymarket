# 18tA canonical HKO publication availability

**PASS**

## Convention

A verified observed publication timestamp is used when supplied with evidence. Otherwise the analytical embargo is 14:00 HKT on the first Monday-Friday after settlement that is not a gazetted Hong Kong general holiday.

The fallback is not represented as an exact-publication claim.

## Counts

- Settlement dates: 103
- Verified observed publication timestamps: 0
- Analytical fallback timestamps: 103
- Dates whose fallback crosses a weekend: 32
- Dates whose fallback crosses a gazetted holiday: 11

## Official basis

- HKO Climatological Information Services: data are updated every working day before 2 p.m., up to the previous day.
- GovHK 2026 general holidays: gazetted holiday dates used in the analytical operational calendar.

No final modelling split is assigned. This panel only freezes the outcome-information time used by 18tB.
